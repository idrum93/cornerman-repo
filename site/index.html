Cornerman What this is. A statistical model of UFC fights, trained only on fights that happened before the ones it scores. Each bout gets a probability for the winner and fifteen other markets, with the fair price each implies.

It does not beat the betting market. On 829 fights it had never seen it called 67% of winners against the closing line's 70%, and where they disagreed the market was right more often. That is measured and published, not expected to change.

What it offers instead is a number with its workings shown: which features moved it, what happened historically in fights that looked similar, and a growing record of how often each market's projections come in.
